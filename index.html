Code and documentation for financial analysis

Introduction

I have spent many years working with financial analysis in a professional setting, developing both theoretical insight and, more importantly, practical tools and methods for real-world applications.

This repository is a collection of that experience. Here, I share practical knowledge in the form of programs, models, and structured notes that I have found useful in my work. The focus is on clarity, usability, and applicability rather than purely academic discussion.

Whether you are looking for inspiration, ready-to-use code, or a deeper understanding of financial concepts from a practitioner’s perspective, I hope you will find something of value here.
